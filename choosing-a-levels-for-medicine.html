Choosing A-Levels for Medicine

By The ProjectX Team

Most medical schools want to see Chemistry, plus at least one other science or Maths, but the exact combinations accepted — and how strictly they're enforced — vary between universities.

Choosing A-Levels too narrowly, or without checking specific course requirements, can limit options later in the application process. This guide will cover which combinations keep medicine open without closing off other paths.

Guidance here links to official sources, UCAS, UCAT, and individual medical schools, where relevant. Always check current entry requirements directly with the university for your child's specific application year. These guides support your research, they don't replace an admissions office's decision. Spotted something out of date? Get in touch and we'll fix it.
