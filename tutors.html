Our Tutors

Every tutor is held to the same standard

Rather than a handful of named profiles, we want you to trust the bar every ProjectX tutor has to clear — because that bar doesn't move as we grow.

  • Personally vetted

    Every tutor goes through an interview and subject-knowledge check before joining ProjectX — we don't take applications at face value.

  • Top grades, proven ability

    Our tutors achieved strong A*/A grades (or equivalent) in the subjects they teach, so they know the specification from the inside.

  • Current students or working professionals

    Many are studying at leading UK universities, or already working in their field — bringing an up-to-date, practical view of what success actually requires.

  • UK curriculum fluent

    Comfortable across AQA, Edexcel and OCR specifications, so sessions map directly onto what a student is being taught and examined on.

  • Safeguarding-aware

    Every tutor is personally vetted and briefed on ProjectX's safeguarding practices before working with any student.

Growing carefully

A small, carefully chosen team — for now

We're deliberately keeping the tutor team small while ProjectX gets started, so every match is a considered one rather than whoever's next in a queue. As more tutors join, each one is held to exactly the same standard above — no exceptions.
