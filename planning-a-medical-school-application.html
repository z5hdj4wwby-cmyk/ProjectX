Planning a Medical School Application: A Year-by-Year Guide

By The ProjectX Team

A strong medicine application is built over several years, not in the six months before submission — GCSE choices, work experience and UCAT preparation all compound over time rather than happening at the last minute.

This guide will break the journey down year by year, from Year 10 through to results day, so nothing important gets left until it's too late to fix.

Guidance here links to official sources, UCAS, UCAT, and individual medical schools, where relevant. Always check current entry requirements directly with the university for your child's specific application year. These guides support your research, they don't replace an admissions office's decision. Spotted something out of date? Get in touch and we'll fix it.
