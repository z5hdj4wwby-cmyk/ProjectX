Is Medicine the Right Goal? How to Tell

By The ProjectX Team

Medicine is a long, demanding course and career, and it's worth testing the decision honestly before committing years of study to the application process that leads into it.

This guide will walk through the questions worth asking — about motivation, the day-to-day realities of the job, and how to explore it properly before applying — rather than assuming the answer is already obvious.

Guidance here links to official sources, UCAS, UCAT, and individual medical schools, where relevant. Always check current entry requirements directly with the university for your child's specific application year. These guides support your research, they don't replace an admissions office's decision. Spotted something out of date? Get in touch and we'll fix it.
