University Admissions

Admissions coaching from mentors who've just been through it

Medicine applications move fast and the process changes every cycle. Our coaches are recent applicants themselves — often current medical students — so the advice is current, specific, and grounded in what actually happened in their own UCAT, personal statement and interviews.

Enquire Now

UCAT Preparation

Structured practice across verbal reasoning, decision making, quantitative reasoning, abstract reasoning and situational judgement, with strategy for working under serious time pressure.

Personal Statement Support

One-to-one drafting support to help a student find their genuine angle — not a template — then refine it into a clear, well-structured statement.

Interview & MMI Coaching

Realistic mock interviews and MMI-style stations covering ethics, motivation and communication, with honest, specific feedback after every run-through.

Why it works

Coached by people who did this last cycle, not last decade

Admissions criteria, UCAT question styles and interview formats shift year to year. Our mentors are chosen because they've recently navigated exactly what your child is about to face — so the guidance reflects how medical schools are actually assessing candidates right now, not how they used to.

Recent, first-hand experience

Many of our mentors are current medical students or recent successful applicants, sharing what genuinely helped them.

Specific, honest feedback

No generic scripts — feedback is tailored to how a student actually communicates and thinks under pressure.

Structured, not one-off

Sessions build progressively toward UCAT sitting, submission deadlines and interview season.

Our Tutors

Every tutor is held to the same standard

Rather than a handful of named profiles, we want you to trust the bar every ProjectX tutor has to clear — because that bar doesn't move as we grow.

  • Personally vetted

    Every tutor goes through an interview and subject-knowledge check before joining ProjectX — we don't take applications at face value.

  • Top grades, proven ability

    Our tutors achieved strong A*/A grades (or equivalent) in the subjects they teach, so they know the specification from the inside.

  • Current students or working professionals

    Many are studying at leading UK universities, or already working in their field — bringing an up-to-date, practical view of what success actually requires.

  • UK curriculum fluent

    Comfortable across AQA, Edexcel and OCR specifications, so sessions map directly onto what a student is being taught and examined on.

  • Safeguarding-aware

    Every tutor is personally vetted and briefed on ProjectX's safeguarding practices before working with any student.

Growing carefully

A small, carefully chosen team — for now

We're deliberately keeping the tutor team small while ProjectX gets started, so every match is a considered one rather than whoever's next in a queue. As more tutors join, each one is held to exactly the same standard above — no exceptions.

Our Promise

We stand behind the results

We're confident in what consistent, well-matched tutoring can do. If your child's predicted grade hasn't moved within three months of regular sessions, we'll carry on supporting them at no extra cost until it does.

Why we can offer this

Every match is deliberate — right tutor, right level, right exam board — so progress is the expected outcome, not a hopeful one.

What "regular sessions" means

Weekly 1:1 sessions attended consistently over the three-month period, with any tutor recommendations between sessions followed.

How it works in practice

If a predicted grade hasn't moved, get in touch — we'll review progress with the tutor and continue sessions at no extra cost until it does.
