Building Work Experience for Medicine Without Connections

By The ProjectX Team

Not every applicant has a family connection to healthcare, and admissions tutors are well aware of this — what matters most is what a student has genuinely learned from whatever experience they can access.

From volunteering to shadowing to part-time care work, there are several realistic routes into relevant experience that don't depend on who you know. This guide will cover where to start looking and how to reflect well on what you find.

Guidance here links to official sources, UCAS, UCAT, and individual medical schools, where relevant. Always check current entry requirements directly with the university for your child's specific application year. These guides support your research, they don't replace an admissions office's decision. Spotted something out of date? Get in touch and we'll fix it.
