Our Promise

We stand behind the results

We're confident in what consistent, well-matched tutoring can do. If your child's predicted grade hasn't moved within three months of regular sessions, we'll carry on supporting them at no extra cost until it does.

Why we can offer this

Every match is deliberate — right tutor, right level, right exam board — so progress is the expected outcome, not a hopeful one.

What "regular sessions" means

Weekly 1:1 sessions attended consistently over the three-month period, with any tutor recommendations between sessions followed.

How it works in practice

If a predicted grade hasn't moved, get in touch — we'll review progress with the tutor and continue sessions at no extra cost until it does.

Ready to get started?

Tell us what your child needs help with and we'll take it from there.
