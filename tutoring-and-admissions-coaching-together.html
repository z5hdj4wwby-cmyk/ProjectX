How Tutoring and Admissions Coaching Work Together

By The ProjectX Team

Grades and admissions readiness aren't separate problems to solve one after another — a student who is stretched thin academically will struggle to also prepare well for UCAT and interviews.

This guide will explain how ProjectX's subject tutoring and admissions coaching are designed to work together as one coordinated plan, rather than competing for a student's limited time and attention.

Guidance here links to official sources, UCAS, UCAT, and individual medical schools, where relevant. Always check current entry requirements directly with the university for your child's specific application year. These guides support your research, they don't replace an admissions office's decision. Spotted something out of date? Get in touch and we'll fix it.
