University Prep Coaching

From £40/hour

Medicine-focused admissions coaching from mentors who've recently been through the process themselves.

Areas covered

UCAT Preparation Personal Statement Interview & MMI

Our approach

Our medical mentors are current or recent medical students who know exactly what MMI stations are looking for, drawing on real interview scenarios to prepare your child for the questions universities actually ask. Because they applied recently, the guidance reflects how medical schools are assessing candidates right now — not how they used to.

What's included

UCAT Preparation

Structured practice across verbal reasoning, decision making, quantitative reasoning, abstract reasoning and situational judgement.

Personal Statement Support

One-to-one drafting support to find a genuine angle, then refine it into a clear, well-structured statement.

Interview & MMI Coaching

Realistic mock interviews and MMI-style stations covering ethics, motivation and communication.
